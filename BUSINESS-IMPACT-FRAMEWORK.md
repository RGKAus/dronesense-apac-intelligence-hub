# Business Impact Framework

## 5 — Critical
Immediate action recommended: relevant tender, major BVLOS/DFR rule change, strategic funding, existing-customer impact or major competitor win. Review the same business day and assign an owner.

## 4 — High
Strong commercial or regulatory relevance: DFR/BVLOS trial, aircraft declaration, major integration, fleet expansion or material consultation. Review within one working day.

## 3 — Medium
Worth reading and tracking: operationally relevant product release, agency expansion, standards activity or practical research. Review in the normal intelligence cycle.

## 2 — Low
General awareness: conference, webinar, minor release or low-priority corporate update. No immediate action.

## 1 — Informational
Background, opinion, historical reference or weak early signal. Keep only where it adds useful context.

## Scoring questions
1. Does it create or change an opportunity?
2. Does it affect a customer or strategic account?
3. Does it alter regulation or programme viability?
4. Does it reveal competitor movement?
5. Does it identify funding or procurement timing?
6. Does it require customer or internal communication?
